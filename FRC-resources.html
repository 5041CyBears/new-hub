5041 FRC RESOURCE LIBRARY

FIRST Robotics Competition Resources

A curated library of 53 online resources for students, mentors, coaches, and teams, organized by type and selected for long-term usefulness.

Evergreen first. This page favors stable documentation hubs, learning sites, tools, and community references that remain useful across seasons. For game rules, robot legality, award criteria, and deadlines, always defer to the current official FIRST competition manual and team updates.

Core resources

WPILib Documentation The primary FRC control-system and programming reference, including wiring, software installation, simulation, commands, controls, and tools. FRCDesign.org A comprehensive Onshape-centered learning site for FRC CAD, design fundamentals, mechanism examples, and best practices. Spectrum 3847 Training Student training curriculum covering FRC basics, controls, CAD, build, strategy, media, and awards. Onshape for FIRST Robotics FIRST-specific Onshape resources, FRC parts libraries, field models, KitBot CAD, learning materials, and team setup guidance. The Blue Alliance Community-standard FRC team and event database with match results, videos, rankings, awards, historical data, and notifications. Chief Delphi Searchable community discussion covering nearly every FRC technical and organizational topic.
