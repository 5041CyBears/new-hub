After reporting

What Happens Next?

1

Report

The concern is received through an accessible channel and immediate safety needs are identified.

2

Observe

An appropriate adult listens without judgment and documents what is being reported rather than deciding the outcome immediately.

3

Assess

Qualified adults review severity, frequency, context, impact, safety, available evidence, and whether school procedures must control.

4

Respond

The appropriate support, protective action, parent/guardian involvement, school referral, or other response is used.

Student leader boundary: Student ROAR Leaders may listen and connect a student with an adult. They do not investigate, interview witnesses independently, decide guilt or discipline, or promise confidentiality.
You have options

Other Ways to Report

Talk to a ROAR Mentor Representative

You can report directly to an adult ROAR representative rather than using the website.

Talk to the faculty advisor or a school adult

Especially use a school reporting channel when the concern involves harassment, discrimination, sexual misconduct, threats, or safety.

Talk to a Student ROAR Leader

A student leader can listen and help connect you to an adult, but the student leader will not investigate the concern.
