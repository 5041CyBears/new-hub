Training and Resources

Interactive training and resrouces for members of the 5041 CyBears FRC and FTC programs.

These modules provide training in team foundations, robot design, technical systems, competition readiness, outreach, and other skills used throughout the robotics program.

Each module includes interactive activities and concludes with a quiz. Passing the quiz unlocks a completion certificate for that module.

FIRST Robotics Competition

The FRC training track covers program foundations, safety, team culture, robot systems, judging, media, fundraising, and other skills used by members of Team 5041.

Recommended starting modules

  • What is FIRST Robotics Competition?
  • Safety & Pit Readiness
  • Creating an Inclusive and Productive Culture

After completing the foundation modules, members can move into technical or team-specific training using the navigation menu.

Online resources

Browse the FRC Resource Library for evergreen technical documentation, CAD and design references, programming tools, scouting resources, awards guidance, and team-management resources.

FIRST Tech Challenge

The FTC training track introduces the program, team safety, team culture, and fundamental FTC robot design concepts.

Recommended starting modules

  • What is FIRST Tech Challenge?
  • Safety & Pit Readiness
  • Creating an Inclusive and Productive Culture
  • Drivetrain Basics

Online resources

Browse the FTC Resource Library for evergreen robot-building, CAD, programming, autonomous, vision, awards, scouting, and team-development resources.

Training Certificates

Each module includes a knowledge check at the end. Students must reach the required passing score before the module certificate becomes available.

Completed modules are marked with a check next to their name in the navigation menu on the browser where the module was completed.
