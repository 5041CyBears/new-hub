ROAR Reporting Policy Handbook

Student Support PDF

ROAR — Report, Observe, Assess, Respond — is 5041's team-level reporting and support process for concerns involving safety, conduct, harassment, discrimination, retaliation, team culture, or other issues that need adult attention.

Important: ROAR supports team reporting and response, but it does not replace school policy, Title IX procedures, mandatory reporting requirements, school discipline, or administrative authority.

Where Should I Start?

New student

Know the program

Start with the Program Handbook, complete the required Safety materials, and know how to use ROAR if you need help or need to report a concern.

Student leader

Lead consistently

Use the Program Handbook for role expectations, the Style Guide for public-facing work, and the Annual Reports to understand prior initiatives and documented impact.

Media / outreach / awards

Tell the 5041 story

Use the Style Guide for consistent branding and the Annual Reports for historical evidence, program growth, outreach examples, sponsor information, and impact documentation.

Mentor / coach

Use the right source

Use the Program Handbook, Safety materials, and ROAR as 5041 program references while continuing to defer to current school, district, legal, and FIRST requirements where they control.

Using program materials

Keep the Current Version in Mind

This page is designed to remain a permanent entry point even as individual documents are revised. Program handbooks, safety agreements, season procedures, and similar materials may change from year to year. Always use the most current approved version provided by 5041 or West Branch Community School District.

Policy hierarchy: 5041 documents support the program, but they do not override controlling federal or state law, West Branch Community School District policy or administrative direction, applicable school handbooks, current activity-sharing agreements, or official FIRST requirements.
